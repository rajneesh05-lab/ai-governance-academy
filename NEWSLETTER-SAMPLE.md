## What's new

**What VAR can teach us about agentic testing** — UiPath, 12 October 2026

UiPath describes software-testing agents that execute tests and surface evidence, while people retain judgment, risk decisions, and release accountability.

## How it may impact your daily work

Agentic testing may help quality teams gather test evidence faster and broaden test coverage. It does not transfer responsibility for quality or release decisions away from people; outputs still need validation.

## How / where you could use it

For a low-risk pilot, ask an agent to run a test suite in a non-production environment and summarize failures. Have a tester validate the evidence, record false positives and missed defects, and require a person to approve every release decision.
